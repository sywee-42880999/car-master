## Alias / taxonomy rules found in this batch
- HEAD RESTRAINT is the preferred official master term; HEADREST can remain an alias.
- LATCH LOWER ANCHOR is US terminology; ISOFIX LOWER ANCHOR may appear in global manuals. Do not silently merge without alias metadata.
- SEAT BELT, SEAT BELT BUCKLE, TETHER ANCHOR, LATCH LOWER ANCHOR, and SEAT BELT PRETENSIONER are separate physical components and should remain separate IDs.
- FRONT SEAT / REAR SEAT are parent physical objects; SEATBACK and SEAT CUSHION are subcomponents and may also retain their own IDs.
