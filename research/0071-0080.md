## Alias / duplicate rules

- `SIDE AIRBAG` vs `FRONT SIDE AIRBAG`: do not silently collapse. 2026 Tucson explicitly distinguishes front/rear side airbags; some IONIQ manuals use generic `Side airbag`.
- `PASSENGER'S FRONT AIRBAG` and `FRONT PASSENGER AIRBAG ON/OFF SWITCH` are different physical items and must never share an image.
- 0077 should teach the control switch, not the hidden warmer control module/heating element.
- 0078 is REVIEW until we decide whether the master should represent the feature (`AIR VENTILATION SEAT`) or the visible control (`AIR VENTILATION SEAT SWITCH`).
- 0079 and 0080 are not duplicates: lever is on/near the seat; remote folding button is a separate cargo-area control where equipped.
- Existing 0068/0069 child-restraint anchors are not reused in this batch.
