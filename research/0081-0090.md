## Alias / duplicate rules

- 0081 `INSIDE REARVIEW MIRROR` vs existing 0004 `SIDE VIEW MIRRORS`: different physical mirror locations; never share image.
- 0082 `DIGITAL CENTER MIRROR` is not an alias of 0081 for learning purposes because the display-equipped module is visually/functionally distinct. Keep 0081 as conventional optical mirror term and 0082 as digital system/module.
- 0083 `MAP LAMP` and 0084 `ROOM LAMP` may share an overhead console assembly, but they are distinct lamp functions/positions. Final images must isolate the correct lamp/control.
- 0085 `REAR PERSONAL LAMP` and 0084 `ROOM LAMP` remain separate because Hyundai explicitly lists both.
- 0086 `CENTER CONSOLE LAMP` must not be confused with the previously researched `CENTER CONSOLE` physical trim/storage assembly.
- 0087 `MOOD LAMP`: possible alias `AMBIENT LIGHT`/`AMBIENT LIGHTING` on other Hyundai models. Keep alias until model-by-model terminology map is complete; do not silently rename.
- 0088 is the lamp adjacent to the vanity mirror, not the mirror itself and not the whole sun visor.
- 0089 is the lamp inside/near glove box, not GLOVE BOX itself.
- 0090 Hyundai source text says `Cargo area lamp`, while image asset key says `LuggageRoomLamp`; master terminology should follow visible official wording, with `LUGGAGE ROOM LAMP` as possible regional/model alias.
