- 0133 TOWING HOOK ≠ towing-hook hole/cover.
- 0134 master wording follows LX3 `WHEEL LUG NUT WRENCH`; `WHEEL LUG WRENCH` and `WHEEL NUT WRENCH` are aliases unless hardware differs.
- 0136 SPARE TIRE ≠ 0007 TIRES.
