## Duplicate / alias QA

- 0145 FUSE BOX conflicts conceptually with existing 0040 FUSE BOX. 0040 source is interior fuse box. Do not mark 0145 PASS until naming convention is resolved, e.g. `ENGINE COMPARTMENT FUSE BOX` vs `INTERIOR FUSE BOX`.
- 0144 BATTERY should preserve official LX3 wording but normalize aliases: `12V BATTERY`, `BATTERY (12 V)` where they refer to the low-voltage battery. Do not confuse with EV high-voltage battery system.
- 0141 ENGINE COOLANT RESERVOIR and EV `COOLANT RESERVOIR` may be physically/functionally different enough to need separate taxonomy. Keep ICE-specific master candidate here.
- 0148 AIR CLEANER ≠ 0150 CABIN AIR FILTER. Engine intake air cleaner and passenger-compartment air filter are different parts.
- `WINDSHIELD` (US) and `WINDSCREEN` (UK) are regional aliases. Use US Hyundai global/manual baseline unless project rule changes.
- `FUSE/RELAY BOX` from older LX2 should be an alias/source note, not automatically a new ID.
