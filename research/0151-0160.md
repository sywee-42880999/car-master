## Candidates

### 0151 ENGINE OIL FILTER / 엔진 오일 필터
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording appears in maintenance schedule as “Engine oil and engine oil filter”.
Visual rule: use a dedicated service illustration or clear close-up of the physical filter. Do not use an oil-filler-cap image; 0142 already covers ENGINE OIL FILLER CAP.

### 0152 DRIVE BELT / 구동 벨트
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording: “Drive belts”.
Master preference: DRIVE BELT if one belt is clearly isolated; alias DRIVE BELTS for maintenance wording.
Visual rule: crop belt path/component itself, not the whole engine bay if avoidable.

### 0153 SPARK PLUG / 점화 플러그
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording: “Spark plugs”.
Master preference: singular for one isolated part image; plural alias for schedule terminology.
Visual rule: dedicated part/service diagram preferred.

### 0154 FUEL FILLER CAP / 연료 주입구 캡
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording in maintenance schedule: “fuel filler cap”.
Distinct from 0012 FUEL FILLER DOOR. Door = exterior flap; cap = sealing cap behind the door.
Visual rule: close-up strongly preferred; no marker if clearly isolated.

### 0155 FUEL TANK / 연료 탱크
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording: “fuel tank”.
Visual rule: underside/service illustration preferred. Avoid generic vehicle exterior image.

### 0156 FUEL TANK AIR FILTER / 연료 탱크 에어 필터
Status: REVIEW
Official maintenance wording exists. Physical location/appearance may be unclear in owner-manual imagery, so do not guess from generic EVAP components.
Require a Hyundai service/owner illustration that names the part directly before PASS.

### 0157 FUEL FILTER / 연료 필터
Status: REVIEW
Official maintenance wording exists. Often maintenance-free and not visually labeled in owner’s manual.
Require a directly labeled Hyundai diagram before production.

### 0158 BRAKE LINE / 브레이크 라인
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording: “Brake lines, hoses, and connections”.
Master rule: BRAKE LINE for a clearly isolated rigid line. Keep BRAKE HOSE separate as another ID if the flexible hose is visually distinguishable.

### 0159 BRAKE HOSE / 브레이크 호스
Status: CANDIDATE
Derived from the same official wording “Brake lines, hoses, and connections”.
Distinct from 0158 by physical construction: flexible hose vs rigid line.
Visual rule: wheel-well/brake close-up or technical illustration.

### 0160 BRAKE PAD / 브레이크 패드
Status: CANDIDATE
Official wording: “Disc brakes and pads”.
Master preference: BRAKE PAD for one isolated pad; plural alias BRAKE PADS.
Visual rule: dedicated close-up/diagram strongly preferred. Do not confuse with brake disc/rotor or caliper.

## Duplicate / taxonomy checks
- 0151 ENGINE OIL FILTER ≠ 0142 ENGINE OIL FILLER CAP ≠ 0147 ENGINE OIL DIPSTICK.
- 0154 FUEL FILLER CAP ≠ 0012 FUEL FILLER DOOR.
- 0158 BRAKE LINE and 0159 BRAKE HOSE are separate physical parts even though Hyundai groups them in one maintenance item.
- 0160 BRAKE PAD should remain separate from future BRAKE DISC/ROTOR and BRAKE CALIPER IDs.
- 0156 and 0157 stay REVIEW until a directly named visual source is verified.

## Image handoff for Codex / Work
1. Prefer original embedded Hyundai manual/service illustrations over rerendered PDF pages.
2. Use a tight crop when the part can stand alone; no red marker needed in that case.
3. If context is required, use HTML overlay only: red dot for a precise small part, soft red oval only for a broad surface.
