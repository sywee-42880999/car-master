- 0181 WHEEL BEARING ≠ 0180 WHEEL HUB ≠ 0008 WHEEL.
- 0182 STRUT ≠ 0175 SHOCK ABSORBER unless a specific source explicitly treats the part that way; preserve architecture distinction.

- 0185 STABILIZER LINK ≠ 0177 STABILIZER BAR.

- 0189 UNDER COVER ≠ skid plate by default.
