## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0191/0192 washer nozzles are not 0146 washer reservoir.
- 0193 WIPER ARM ≠ 0003 FRONT WIPER BLADES.
- 0194 REAR WIPER ARM ≠ 0019 REAR WINDOW WIPER BLADE.
- 0195 HOOD LATCH ≠ 0038 HOOD RELEASE LEVER; one is under-hood latch hardware, the other cabin release control.
- 0196 is secondary safety hardware and stays REVIEW until exact Hyundai terminology is secured.
- 0197 LIFTGATE HANDLE ≠ 0016 LIFTGATE.
- 0198 LIFTGATE STRUT and 0182 suspension STRUT share a word but are entirely different parts; category metadata must prevent confusion.
- 0199 DOOR HINGE ≠ 0200 DOOR CHECKER.
