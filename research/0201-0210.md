## Candidate IDs

### 0201 — LICENSE PLATE LIGHT / 번호판등
Status: CANDIDATE
- Exterior rear illumination for license plate.
- Keep distinct from rear combination lights and reverse lights.

### 0202 — REFLECTOR / 리플렉터
Status: REVIEW

- Require exact model-specific official wording before PASS.

### 0203 — SIDE REPEATER / 사이드 리피터
Status: REVIEW
- Direction-indicator repeater can be mounted in mirror or fender depending model.
- Regional Hyundai terminology may vary; exact source required.

### 0204 — SIDE MARKER LIGHT / 사이드 마커 램프
Status: REVIEW
- Market-specific item, especially North America.
- Do not merge with turn signal or position light without official source confirmation.

### 0205 — DAYTIME RUNNING LIGHT / 주간주행등
Status: CANDIDATE
- Hyundai official manuals commonly label this as Daytime running light.
- Keep separate from position light and turn signal even when functions share one lamp housing.

### 0206 — POSITION LIGHT / 미등
Status: CANDIDATE
- Distinct lighting function from DRL and turn signal.

### 0207 — FRONT PARKING SENSOR / 전방 주차 센서

### 0208 — REAR PARKING SENSOR / 후방 주차 센서

### 0209 — SIDE VIEW MIRROR TURN SIGNAL / 실외 미러 방향지시등

### 0210 — DOOR SCUFF TRIM / 도어 스커프 트림
Status: REVIEW

## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0201 LICENSE PLATE LIGHT is not part of 0014 REAR COMBINATION LIGHTS.
- 0202 REFLECTOR is passive reflective hardware, not an illuminated lamp.
- 0203 SIDE REPEATER and 0209 SIDE VIEW MIRROR TURN SIGNAL may overlap on mirror-mounted designs; preserve only physically distinct items/labels supported by Hyundai documentation.
- 0205 DRL, 0206 POSITION LIGHT and 0028 TURN SIGNAL LIGHT may coexist in one lamp cluster but are separate functions.
- 0207/0208 may ultimately become aliases/children under 0027 PARKING DISTANCE WARNING SENSOR; do not duplicate blindly.
- 0210 nomenclature needs model-specific source control.
