# CAR MASTER Research — IDs 0221–0230

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Do not increase Production completion until final learning images are sourced/cropped, QA’d, bound to permanent IDs, and deployed in BLACK UI.

Primary terminology policy: Hyundai official Owner’s Manual wording first. This batch focuses on exterior body openings / seals / trim where image-based learning can still be clear.

## Candidate IDs

### 0221 — DOOR WEATHERSTRIP / 도어 웨더스트립
Status: CANDIDATE
- Rubber sealing strip around door/body opening.
- Close-up of door opening preferred.

### 0222 — WINDOW WEATHERSTRIP / 윈도우 웨더스트립
Status: REVIEW
- Can overlap visually/terminologically with belt molding and glass run channel.
- Require Hyundai exact label and clear image before PASS.

### 0223 — DOOR SILL / 도어 실
Status: CANDIDATE
- Lower body threshold at door opening.
- Distinguish from 0210 DOOR SCUFF TRIM, which is trim covering the sill area.

### 0224 — ROCKER PANEL / 로커 패널
Status: CANDIDATE
- Exterior lower body side structure below doors.
- Distinguish structural panel from garnish/cladding.

### 0225 — SIDE SILL GARNISH / 사이드 실 가니쉬
Status: REVIEW
- Exterior trim/cladding over lower side sill area.
- Naming varies by model; requires official Hyundai label.

### 0226 — WHEEL ARCH CLADDING / 휠 아치 클래딩
Status: CANDIDATE
- Exterior protective/decorative trim around wheel opening, common on SUV models.
- Keep separate from fender body panel.

### 0227 — FENDER / 펜더
Status: CANDIDATE
- Exterior body panel around front wheel opening.
- Use vehicle-angle crop where panel boundaries are understandable.

### 0228 — QUARTER PANEL / 쿼터 패널
Status: CANDIDATE
- Rear side body panel around rear wheel / quarter area.
- Distinguish from QUARTER GLASS 0213.

### 0229 — COWL TOP COVER / 카울 탑 커버
Status: REVIEW
- Plastic/trim area at base of windshield, often around wiper pivots.
- Exact Hyundai model terminology needs confirmation.

### 0230 — WINDSHIELD MOLDING / 앞유리 몰딩
Status: REVIEW
- Trim/seal around windshield perimeter.
- Distinguish from WINDSHIELD 0024 and cowl components.

## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0221 DOOR WEATHERSTRIP and 0222 WINDOW WEATHERSTRIP are separate sealing functions but may be ambiguously labeled in some manuals.
- 0223 DOOR SILL ≠ 0210 DOOR SCUFF TRIM.
- 0224 ROCKER PANEL ≠ 0225 SIDE SILL GARNISH; structural body vs exterior trim.
- 0226 WHEEL ARCH CLADDING ≠ 0227 FENDER.
- 0228 QUARTER PANEL ≠ 0213 QUARTER GLASS.
- 0229 COWL TOP COVER ≠ 0193 WIPER ARM and ≠ 0024 WINDSHIELD.
- 0230 WINDSHIELD MOLDING ≠ windshield glass itself.
