# CAR MASTER Research — IDs 0251–0260

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Production completion does not increase until final images are produced, QA’d, bound to permanent IDs and deployed in BLACK UI.

Primary terminology policy: Hyundai official Owner’s Manual terminology first. This batch moves into HVAC / dashboard air-distribution components that can be visually learned from interior diagrams and close-ups.

## Candidate IDs

### 0251 — FRONT AIR VENT / 프론트 에어 벤트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Dashboard air outlet/vent.
- Prefer a clearly isolated vent rather than the entire climate-control panel.

### 0252 — REAR AIR VENT / 리어 에어 벤트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Rear passenger air outlet, often on rear of center console or roof depending model.
- Model-specific placement must be preserved.

### 0253 — DEFROSTER VENT / 디프로스터 벤트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Windshield-directed HVAC outlet.
- Distinguish from side window defroster outlet where manuals separate them.

### 0254 — SIDE AIR VENT / 사이드 에어 벤트
Status: REVIEW
- Potential taxonomy overlap with 0251 FRONT AIR VENT.
- Keep separate only if Hyundai source identifies side/outer vent distinctly.

### 0255 — CLIMATE CONTROL PANEL / 공조 조절 패널
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical HVAC control interface.
- Distinguish from 0053 CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM, which is system-level terminology.

### 0256 — TEMPERATURE CONTROL KNOB / 온도 조절 노브
Status: REVIEW
- Interface varies by model: knob, button, touch control.
- Use only for models with an actual rotary knob and exact Hyundai wording.

### 0257 — FAN SPEED CONTROL / 풍량 조절부
Status: REVIEW
- Control implementation varies by model.
- Final taxonomy may use button/knob-specific term instead of abstract control.

### 0258 — AIR INTAKE CONTROL BUTTON / 내외기 전환 버튼
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical button controlling recirculation/outside-air intake.
- Use official Hyundai wording for final card.

### 0259 — FRONT WINDSHIELD DEFROSTER BUTTON / 앞유리 서리제거 버튼
Status: CANDIDATE
- Dedicated front windshield defrost/defog control.
- Distinguish from actual 0253 DEFROSTER VENT.

### 0260 — REAR WINDOW DEFROSTER BUTTON / 뒷유리 열선 버튼
Status: CANDIDATE
- Dedicated rear-window defroster control.
- Do not confuse control button with heating grid embedded in rear glass.

## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0251 FRONT AIR VENT vs 0254 SIDE AIR VENT: possible hierarchy/overlap; 0254 remains REVIEW.
- 0255 CLIMATE CONTROL PANEL ≠ 0053 CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM: physical interface vs whole system.
- 0256/0257 must reflect actual hardware type; do not force knob terminology onto touch/button systems.
- 0259 DEFROSTER BUTTON ≠ 0253 DEFROSTER VENT.
- 0260 REAR WINDOW DEFROSTER BUTTON is a control, not the rear-window heating element.
