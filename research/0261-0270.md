## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0261 mechanical adjustment lever ≠ 0262 power adjustment switch.
- 0266 LANE SAFETY BUTTON may overlap 0097 LANE DRIVING ASSIST BUTTON; source verification required before PASS.
- 0267 POWER LIFTGATE BUTTON ≠ 0016 LIFTGATE and ≠ 0197 LIFTGATE HANDLE.
- 0268 is only valid where a physical fuel-door release control exists; 0012 is the door itself.
- 0269 is a control; 0013 is the charging door.
- 0270 HEAD-UP DISPLAY ≠ 0050 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER.
