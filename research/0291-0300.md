## Candidate IDs

### 0291 — USB DATA PORT / USB 데이터 포트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Data-capable USB connection used for infotainment/phone connectivity.
- Distinguish from charge-only USB ports where manuals separate them.

### 0292 — USB CHARGING PORT / USB 충전 포트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Charge-only or charging-designated USB port.
- Potential overlap with 0045 USB CHARGER; final taxonomy should separate charger module vs physical port only if official source supports it.

### 0293 — 12 V POWER OUTLET / 12V 전원 소켓
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical 12 V socket/outlet.
- Potential overlap with 0046 POWER OUTLET; keep exact physical terminology model-specific.

### 0294 — AC POWER OUTLET / AC 전원 콘센트
Status: REVIEW
- Only for models equipped with AC outlet/inverter output socket.
- Distinguish from 0060 AC INVERTER system/module.

### 0295 — WIRELESS CHARGING PAD / 무선 충전 패드
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical charging pad surface.
- Distinguish from 0047 WIRELESS SMARTPHONE CHARGING SYSTEM, which is system-level.

### 0296 — CUP HOLDER INSERT / 컵홀더 인서트
Status: REVIEW
- Removable insert/divider terminology varies by model.

### 0297 — CENTER CONSOLE ARMREST / 센터 콘솔 암레스트
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical armrest/lid area of the center console.
- Distinguish from 0042 CENTER CONSOLE and 0111 CENTER CONSOLE STORAGE.

### 0298 — CONSOLE STORAGE LID / 콘솔 수납함 덮개
Status: REVIEW

- Exact Hyundai wording required.

### 0299 — PARKING BRAKE PEDAL / 주차 브레이크 페달
Status: REVIEW
- Relevant only to vehicles with foot-operated parking brake.
- Do not confuse with 0037 EPB switch or generic parking brake system.

### 0300 — BRAKE PEDAL / 브레이크 페달
Status: CANDIDATE
- Physical service-brake pedal.
- Clear isolated close-up preferred.
