# CAR MASTER Research — IDs 0321–0330

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Production progress changes only after final image production, QA, permanent-ID binding and BLACK UI deployment.

Primary terminology policy: Hyundai official Owner’s Manual wording first. Batch theme: seat belt and child-restraint hardware.

## Candidate IDs

### 0321 — SEAT BELT HEIGHT ADJUSTER / 시트벨트 높이 조절장치
Status: CANDIDATE
- Upper anchor height adjustment mechanism.
- Distinguish from 0066 SEAT BELT and 0070 PRETENSIONER.

### 0322 — SEAT BELT ANCHOR / 시트벨트 앵커
Status: CANDIDATE
- Fixed/upper belt anchoring point or mounting hardware.
- Source image must clearly identify the anchor hardware.

### 0323 — SEAT BELT TONGUE / 시트벨트 텅
Status: CANDIDATE
- Metal latch plate inserted into buckle.
- Distinguish from 0067 SEAT BELT BUCKLE.

### 0324 — SEAT BELT RETRACTOR / 시트벨트 리트랙터
Status: CANDIDATE
- Belt reel/retraction mechanism.
- Often hidden behind trim; use official diagram rather than generic cabin photo.

### 0325 — CHILD RESTRAINT ANCHORAGE / 어린이 보호장치 앵커리지
Status: REVIEW
- Umbrella wording may overlap 0068 TETHER ANCHOR and 0069 LATCH LOWER ANCHOR.
- Keep only if Hyundai source uses this as a distinct physical grouping.

### 0326 — TOP TETHER ANCHORAGE / 탑 테더 앵커리지
Status: CANDIDATE
- Child-restraint upper tether anchorage.
- Potential naming overlap with 0068 TETHER ANCHOR; taxonomy consolidation required before PASS.

### 0327 — LOWER ANCHORAGE / 하부 앵커리지
Status: CANDIDATE
- ISOFIX/LATCH lower anchorage point.
- Potential overlap with 0069 LATCH LOWER ANCHOR; verify source wording and merge policy.

### 0328 — CHILD RESTRAINT ANCHOR COVER / 어린이 보호장치 앵커 커버
Status: REVIEW
- Physical trim/cover over anchorage where separately identified.
- Model-specific and may not warrant a standalone permanent ID.

### 0329 — SEAT BELT BUCKLE RELEASE BUTTON / 시트벨트 버클 해제 버튼
Status: CANDIDATE
- Release button integrated into the buckle.
- Subcomponent of 0067 SEAT BELT BUCKLE; retain only if subcomponent-level learning is desired.

### 0330 — SEAT BELT GUIDE / 시트벨트 가이드
Status: REVIEW
- Hyundai wording and physical form vary by seat design.
- Do not confuse with height adjuster or anchor.

## Duplicate / taxonomy QA

- 0321 height adjuster ≠ 0322 anchor ≠ 0324 retractor.
- 0323 tongue ≠ 0067 buckle; both should be visually distinct.
- 0325 may be too broad because 0068 and 0069 already exist.
- 0326 likely overlaps 0068 TETHER ANCHOR; do not PASS both until exact taxonomy is resolved.
- 0327 likely overlaps 0069 LATCH LOWER ANCHOR; do not duplicate the same physical anchor under two names.
- 0329 is a subcomponent of 0067 and should be retained only if the wordbook includes subcomponent controls.
